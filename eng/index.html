JDG FAQ

Which should I choose: Ryczałt, Liniowy or Skala?+

It depends on your type of activity (PKD code), income level and costs. Ryczałt often works well with low costs, Liniowy with high income and significant costs, and Skala with moderate income. We calculate every option, including składka zdrowotna, and recommend the most cost-effective one for your situation.

Can I change my tax form?+

Yes, usually from the new tax year: the application is filed via CEIDG by the 20th of the month following the month of your first income in the year. We'll calculate in advance whether switching pays off and file the application on time.

What are Ulga na start and Mały ZUS Plus?+

Ulga na start means the first 6 months of a new business without ZUS social contributions (only składka zdrowotna is paid). After that, preferential ZUS applies for 24 months, and then — if you stay within the income limit — you can switch to Mały ZUS Plus. We'll check which reliefs you're entitled to and arrange the switch on time.

Can I switch accountants mid-year?+

Yes. We request the KPiR or Ewidencja przychodów and documents from your previous accountant, review the PIT, ZUS and VAT calculations, and continue bookkeeping from the month of transfer without any gaps.

How do you work with KSeF?+

KSeF support is included in the service: we give you access to an invoicing system, help set up KSeF access, and incoming e-invoices flow automatically into your bookkeeping.

I'm only planning to open a JDG. Where do I start?+

With a consultation: we'll pick PKD codes, compare tax forms, tell you whether you need VAT registration and explain which ZUS reliefs are available to you. That way you start without mistakes that are expensive to fix later.
